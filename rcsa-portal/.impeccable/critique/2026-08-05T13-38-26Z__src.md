## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Grounded in the domain at the data layer and in two widgets; category-interchangeable in almost everything else. The vocabulary is genuinely this product's. But composition is a template applied twelve times — `VulnsPage` and `FindingsPage` are the same file with nouns swapped, and eleven modules share a byte-identical empty-state string. Two widgets escape and are the best things in the product: the **SLA runway** (`VulnsPage.tsx:187`) and the **Source × Severity matrix** (`FindingsPage.tsx:174`) — both encode a decision rather than a distribution. The shell has no product character; "Force Re-Sync" on localStorage data is SaaS cosplay that contradicts the product's own no-security-theater principle. Biggest miss: the north star is *documents → service*, yet no empty state anywhere says "import your spreadsheet."

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs` exit 2, **10 findings**, all `warning`: `gray-on-color` ×6 (`AssetSummaryModal.tsx:154,179–183`), `side-tab` ×4 (`index.css:84`, `MasterCalendarPage.tsx:62`, `CompliancePage.tsx:217`, `ReportsPage.tsx:235`). **Nearly all are false positives** — the gray-on-color hits reason about literal Tailwind classes that `index.css:62-72` remaps to tokens; three side-tab hits are row/list markers, not card accents; one is the print document. ## Overall Impression

The engineering discipline here is better than the interface. Honest-data rules are enforced in the component layer, the token system is real, and two widgets are genuinely excellent. But the product's own promise — get off spreadsheets — breaks at exactly the moment it matters: an analyst mid-audit still ends in Excel, because she can't sort by due date, can't filter to overdue, and can't scope a report. **The single biggest opportunity: make the dashboard's drill-through carry the record it already identified, and make registers sortable.** Those two changes convert the app from a viewer into a tool.

## What's Working

1. **The two signature widgets earn their place.** SLA runway and the Source × Severity matrix both encode a *decision*. The runway's centre line is "today", so overdue reads as physical position, not a colour you decode. The matrix reproduces the exact cross-tab an auditor asks for and every cell is a filter — the artifact and the navigation are the same object.
2. **Honest-data discipline is enforced in code, not policy.** `KpiCard` renders a delta only when a prior snapshot exists; `DonutChart` returns "No data yet" rather than a fake ring; the Demo badge and login disclaimer state limitations plainly. Most products claiming this enforce it in a doc.
3. **The cross-filter chip pattern is uniform and always reversible** across eleven modules — chip in the panel header *and* the toolbar, empty state names the cause, one click clears.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Drill-through drops the record and wipes the user's filters.**
Every cross-module link navigates to a *module*, never a *record* (`widgets.tsx:148,186,243`; bell at `AppShell.tsx:305`), and `AppShell.tsx:105` clears filters on every module switch.
*Why it matters:* This breaks the core promise. You click "TRT-014 — Overdue 12d" and land on an unfiltered page 1 holding an ID in your head, with your query destroyed. The aggregation layer computes the exact record, then throws that identity away.
*Fix:* Extend the existing `lib/intent.ts` seam (it already carries `new:vulns`) to carry `open:VUL-014`; each page reads it in its existing `takeIntent` initializer. Stop clearing filters unless the target shares the namespace.
*Command:* `/impeccable shape` (navigation model), then `/impeccable harden`

**[P0] Destructive actions are mislabeled and undefended.**
`UiProvider.tsx:72` hardcodes the confirm button to **"Delete"** regardless of action — so "Reset demo data?", which discards every change in a module, is confirmed by a button reading "Delete". It sits in the standard toolbar beside "+ New Finding". No undo exists anywhere.
*Why it matters:* In a register of regulator findings an accidental reset is unrecoverable and may not even be noticed. The button label actively misdescribes the act — the one thing a confirm dialog exists to prevent.
*Fix:* Add `confirmLabel` to `ui.confirm` and use the real verb; move "Reset demo data" into Settings; add a 10s undo to the delete toast (the repos already return the removed record).
*Command:* `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Forms are unusable with a screen reader — 157 controls have no programmatic label.**
`htmlFor` appears **zero times** in the codebase; 120 `<label>` elements neither wrap a control nor associate with one. 157 inputs/selects/textareas have neither label nor `aria-label` (87 rely on placeholder only). Add: no focus trap or focus restore in `Modal.tsx`, **no `:focus-visible` rule anywhere**, the confirm dialog has no Escape handler and no `aria-labelledby`, and table rows are `<tr onClick>` with no `tabIndex` or key handler.
*Why it matters:* For a financial institution this is procurement-blocking, and it makes an entire class of colleague unable to do the primary task. Note the counterpoint: icon *buttons* are well handled — only 2 of 182 lack a name.
*Fix:* Wrap controls in `<label>` (three helpers already do this correctly — `AssetFilters.tsx:17`, `DashboardSlicer.tsx:9`, `MasterCalendarPage.tsx:297`); add one global `:focus-visible` rule; add focus trap + restore to `Modal`; make the row's first cell a real button.
*Command:* `/impeccable audit`, then `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Severity — the most consequential datum — is the least legible text on screen.**
Light theme, computed and verified twice: `--med` on `--med-bg` **2.67:1**, `--low` **2.90**, `--high` **3.13**, `--info` **3.52**, `--crit` **4.15**, `--mut` **4.20** — all below AA 4.5. `--text3` on `--surface` **3.43** and it carries 286 usages (table headers, placeholders, timestamps, empty states). Primary button white-on-accent **3.74**. **Dark theme pills all pass (5.2–8.2)** — this is a light-theme failure. Worst case: dark-theme heat-map cells render `#e9f1f0` text on fixed light pastels at **1.04–1.18:1** — effectively invisible (`HeatmapModal.tsx:8-11`).
*Fix:* Darken `--med`→~`#a16207`, `--low`→~`#15803d`, `--text3`→~`#5f7270` for foreground use (keep current values for fills). Give HeatmapModal themed cell colours. All token-file edits; nothing downstream changes.
*Command:* `/impeccable colorize` (token contrast pass)

**[P2] Registers bury the register and can't be sorted.**
All 12 pages put 4 stat cards + 2–3 charts + toolbar above the table; only `IncidentList` sorts. Findings computes an overdue count for a stat card but gives no way to filter or sort by it.
*Why it matters:* A spreadsheet replacement where you can't click a column header to sort by due date is a downgrade with charts on top.
*Fix:* Add a shared `useSortableTable` beside `usePaged`; make stat cards clickable filters (they already carry the predicates); allow collapsing the chart band with state persisted like panel order.
*Command:* `/impeccable layout`

**[P3] Ship-blocking small defects on executive surfaces.** The "6 of " string, the frozen sync time, "Force Re-Sync" on non-syncing data, and four `.css.bak` files still in `src/`. Individually trivial; collectively what a CISO notices in the first thirty seconds.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (impatient power user):** Cannot link to anything — no router exists, so he can't bookmark Vulnerabilities or paste a finding into Teams; for a product built to kill email threads, that's a strategic failure. `⌘K` is bound on one page only and just filters that page's widgets. Can't sort vulns by SLA deadline — the runway chart shows only `.slice(0,8)`. The runway rows aren't clickable (`VulnsPage.tsx:196` are plain `<div>`s) — the widget most likely to trigger action is inert. Twelve modal round-trips to close twelve remediated vulns. Every module switch costs him his query.

**Sam (keyboard / screen reader):** Cannot open a record from a table — `<tr onClick>` with no `tabIndex`/key handler; his only route in is the pencil icon. Cannot sort — `<th onClick>` with no `aria-sort`. Falls out of dialogs — no focus trap, no focus restore, so he lands at document start and re-tabs past 21 nav buttons. The delete confirmation announces nothing and has no Escape. Focus is invisible: no `:focus-visible` anywhere; the input ring is `#d4f2ec` on `#f5f8f7` = **1.11:1**, so a 1px border swap carries the entire indicator. 157 unlabelled form controls. Panel reorder advertises `aria-label="Drag to reorder"` on a button that does nothing via keyboard.

**GRC analyst mid-audit, auditor in the room:** She scrolls past four stat cards and three charts to reach the register — every visit. The NBG × High matrix cell works beautifully. Then she stalls: **cannot sort by due date, cannot filter to overdue.** The auditor asks for the list; Export CSV gives correct filtered rows, but Reports builds a fixed 10-section board pack that **cannot be scoped to "NBG High findings only"** — so she opens the CSV in Excel and formats it by hand. *That is the exact workflow this product exists to eliminate, at the highest-visibility moment of the department's year.* She checks a linked vuln; coming back, her matrix filter is gone. Asked who changed FND-003's due date, she can find it — the audit diff is genuinely impressive — but there's no history affordance on the record itself, so she must memorise the ID and retype it in another module. And opening a finding row drops her straight into an **editable form** with no dirty guard and no undo, mid-audit, under scrutiny.

## Minor Observations

- Two `Panel` components with different type scales (15px `<h2>` vs 13px `<h3>`) render side by side in the same grid on the dashboard.
- The design system is string-matching, not an API: `index.css:57-72` overrides `.bg-blue-600` etc. with `!important`; any new component using an unlisted Tailwind colour silently escapes the theme.
- `html { font-size: 16.5px }` plus ~19 hardcoded font sizes (`text-[12.5px]`, `text-[11.5px]`, `text-[10.5px]`, `text-[9.5px]`) — no type scale, just accumulated one-offs.
- Toast colours bypass tokens entirely (`'bg-orange-600'`, `'bg-green-700'` as strings) and won't follow the theme.
- Mobile: Findings/Vulns hide Source and Owner columns below `md`/`sm`, and row-click is gated on `perms.edit` — so **a read-only user on a phone cannot see those fields at all**.
- Drag-reorder is unreachable below 1024px and for all keyboard and touch users (gated on `onMouseDown`).
- `ChartGrid` keys saved layouts by `slugOf(title)` — renaming a panel silently resets the user's arrangement.
- Collapsed sidebar reuses `shield` for RCSA Dashboard and Directory, `alert` for Risk Catalogue and Vulnerabilities, `doc` for Reports and Policies — three icon collisions in a 21-item rail where the icon is the only label.
- `Pagination` returns `null` at `total === 0`, so the page-size control vanishes and the layout jumps when a filter empties the table.
- "Coming Soon" infrastructure ships with zero planned modules — dead surface area.
- Emoji (🎉, 🖨) in a bank tool.

## Questions to Consider

1. **If the north star is "off spreadsheets," why is the spreadsheet a second-class citizen?** The one sentence that speaks Excel-user language ("Save As → CSV UTF-8") appears *after* they've already found the button. What if every empty register were a drop zone, paste-from-clipboard worked, and "Export this filtered view as PDF" sat beside "Export CSV"?
2. **Twelve registers share one template — but do they share one job?** A vulnerability is a race against a clock; a finding is an obligation to a named external party; a policy is a review cadence. Four mental models in one costume. What if page shape derived from the object's temporal character?
3. **The dashboard exists to say "go here and do this" — so why does clicking it discard everything it just told you?**
4. **What is the "I'm done" moment?** Right now it's a grey rectangle for 2.5 seconds. Closing a P1 incident that ran 47 days feels identical to dismissing a dialog.
